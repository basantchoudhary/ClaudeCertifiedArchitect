Trap gym

The exam's questions are easy to read. The hard part is the answers: every wrong option is a sensible practice that is wrong here. Each page below takes one trap, lets you watch the options play out in a simulator, and ends with exam-style practice.

Most traps come down to one of three mistakes: the right idea at the wrong layer, fixing the symptom instead of the cause, or the right content in the wrong home. Learn to spot them and you can rule out two options on most questions.
