# Beat sheet: "Plan or just do it?" (about 8–10 minutes)

Not a script. Glance at the point, then say it in your own words while showing the page.
Record one beat per take, and pause 2 seconds before restarting a sentence; the fillers get cut in editing.

## 1. The hook (30 s)
- Show the page title.
- Say: this trap runs both ways. Big open change → the wrong answers start editing. Tiny fix → the wrong answers plan, search and ask questions.
- Promise: size the task first, and the right mode follows.

## 2. Picture or kitchen (1 min)
- Scroll to the two boxes.
- Hanging a picture: you just do it. Redoing a kitchen: measure, agree the layout, then knock walls.
- Land it: direct execution = the picture, plan mode = the kitchen.
- The three helpers in one line each: Explore subagent = the person who measures and brings back one sheet; interview = ask the family first; tests first = tape on the floor.

## 3. The diagram (1 min)
- Trace it: the task → "small, settled" → direct execution (tests first sits beside it) or "big or open" → plan mode, which sends long searches to the Explore subagent and gets a summary back.
- Interview sits above the task: it's for when nobody has said what's wanted.
- Point at "nothing changes until you approve".

## 4. The one question (1 min)
- Butter box: "Is the approach already decided, and is the change small?"
- Read the five bullets fast. Then the two word lists: "single validation check" vs "two libraries", "engineers disagree", "not yet chosen".

## 5. Simulator (3 min). The heart of the video
- Typo + plan mode: 9 min, verdict Overbuilt. Then typo + direct execution: 2 min, Right fit. "Same edit, four times slower."
- Validation check + direct execution: 6 min. "The exam guide's own example." Show tests first: 7 min, Fine too: still direct execution.
- Library move + direct execution: point at the pink. 4 h 55 min total, 1 h 15 min of rework, 13 folders edited twice. "Editing first picked the approach by default."
- Library move + plan mode: 3 h 50 min, no rework. Verdict "Best fit (exam answer)". Point out the context meter at 82%.
- Now plan mode + Explore subagent: 3 h 42 min, meter drops to 28%. "Same plan, the long search stayed in the subagent." Verdict "Also right": a good habit, but on the exam the answer is plan mode.
- CSV export + plan mode: 1 h 20 min with 25 min rework, Different problem. "A careful plan built on a guess." Then interview first: 45 min, Right fit.
- Say once: the minutes are illustrative; the shape is what the exam guide says.

## 6. The four traps table (1 min)
- Read the trap names, and match each to a run you just did: typo + plan, library + direct, CSV + plan, library + plan without Explore.

## 7. Practice question 1, live (2 min)
- Read the stem. Point at "The engineer knows the endpoint file and its test file, and the error format already exists".
- Rule out options by naming their trap: overbuilt (plan), overbuilt (Explore), nothing left to ask (interview).
- Click C, open "Think it through", show the mind map.
- Leave questions 2 (Explore subagent) and 3 (tests first) for the viewer: "pause and try them".

## 8. Close (20 s)
- Rule to remember: small and settled → just do it; big or undecided → plan first.
- Point back to the Trap gym page for the next trap.
