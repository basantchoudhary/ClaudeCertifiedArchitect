# Beat sheet: "The coordinator's split" (about 8–10 minutes)

Not a script. Glance at the point, then say it in your own words while showing the page.
Record one beat per take, and pause 2 seconds before restarting a sentence; the fillers get cut in editing.

## 1. The hook (30 s)
- Show the title.
- Say: a multi-agent report comes back wrong, and three of the four answers offer to fix the subagents.
- Promise: one question that tells you whether to fix the subagent or the coordinator.

## 2. The head chef and the tickets (1 min)
- Scroll to the two boxes.
- Dinner with no main course: every cook did their job; the chef never wrote that ticket.
- Soup with bacon: the cook never heard “one guest is vegetarian”; it wasn't on the ticket.
- Land it: chef = coordinator, cook = subagent, ticket = Task prompt. A subagent starts empty.

## 3. The three choices the coordinator makes (1 min)
- Point at the diagram: the split (which topics), the brief (what each knows), the timing (together or in turn).
- The gap check underneath: a safety net, not the cause.
- The subagent strip: “each starts empty and knows only its prompt”.

## 4. The one question (1 min)
- Butter box: “Did each subagent do what it was asked? If yes, the fault is upstream.”
- One example per bullet: missing topics → split; ignored rule → brief; queue → timing; recipe → goals.
- Show the word lists. “Within its topic” and “follow the steps exactly” are the giveaways.

## 5. Simulator (3 min). The heart of the video
- Whole topics missing + Change nothing: 1 of 4 topics, 3 of 12 key studies, 1 min 53 s. Point at the three green bars: all good results, all visual arts.
- + Improve the subagents: sources go from 16 to 25, still 1 of 4 topics, and slower (2 min 25 s). “This is the trap.”
- + Widen the split: 4 of 4, 12 of 12, 2 min 4 s. “Same subagents. Different split.”
- + Add a gap check: also 4 of 4, but 4 min 0 s and a round 2. “Works, but late.”
- The user's rules ignored + Change nothing: 10 of 22 sources older than 2024. Show the Task prompt: no 2024, no museum board.
- + Improve the subagents: down to 5 old sources, never zero. + Put the agreed facts in the Task prompt: 0.
- The run is too slow: 6 min 12 s in a queue; + Send all Task calls in one response: 2 min 4 s.
- Shallow: 4 of 12 key studies by recipe; + Goals and quality criteria: 12 of 12, 2 min 4 s.
- Say once: times and counts are illustrative; the shape is what the exam guide says.

## 6. The traps table (1 min)
- Read the three names: blaming the subagent, catching it late, right layer wrong lever. One simulator example each.

## 7. Practice question 1, live (2 min)
- Read the falls-prevention stem. Point at “exercise programmes, balance training and strength training”: all one kind.
- Point at “well-cited findings within its topic”: the subagents worked.
- Rule out options by trap name: blames the subagent, catches it late, shifts the work to the user.
- Click the answer, open “Think it through”, show the mind map. Leave questions 2 and 3 for the viewer.

## 8. Close (20 s)
- Rule: every subagent did its job and the result is still wrong? Fix the coordinator: the split, the brief or the timing.
- Next trap: “When a tool fails”.
