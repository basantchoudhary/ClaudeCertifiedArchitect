# Beat sheet: "Prompt or hook?" (about 8–10 minutes)

Not a script. Glance at the point, then say it in your own words while showing the page.
Record one beat per take, and pause 2 seconds before restarting a sentence; the fillers get cut in editing.

## 1. The hook (30 s)
- Show the page title.
- Say: this is the trap that cost me marks on the real exam. The question is easy; two answers both look right.
- Promise: one question you can ask that settles a whole family of questions.

## 2. The cashier and the till (1 min)
- Scroll to the two boxes.
- Tell the story: the note by the till (cashier usually remembers) vs the till that won't open over $150.
- Land it: prompt = the note, hook = the till.

## 3. Where each one acts (1 min)
- Point at the diagram, left to right: the prompt guides Claude; PreToolUse checks the call before the tool runs; PostToolUse edits the result before Claude reads it.
- One line to repeat: "Pre stops an action. Post hides data."

## 4. The one question (1 min)
- Show the butter box: "If this goes wrong once, is that acceptable?"
- Walk through the four bullets with one example each: tone, refund, card number, verify-then-refund.
- Show the two word lists. Say: underline these words when you read a question.

## 5. Simulator (3 min). This is the heart of the video
- Refund + prompt instruction: point at the pink cells. "31 refunds that policy didn't allow."
- Refund + prompt + examples: "Better, still not zero. This is the trap answer."
- Refund + PreToolUse: all green. "This is the exam's answer."
- Pause + PreToolUse: yellow cells. "Now the trap in reverse: customers stuck because code misread a chat."
- Card number + PreToolUse: "Right family, wrong hook: the agent can't work at all." Then PostToolUse: all green.
- Say once: the numbers are illustrative; the shape is what the exam guide says.

## 6. The three traps table (1 min)
- Read the trap names only, and give one example each from the simulator you just ran.

## 7. Practice question 1, live (2 min)
- Read the stem out loud. Underline (point at) "a retention idea, not a business rule".
- Rule out options by naming their trap: wrong action, overbuilt, overbuilt.
- Click the answer, then open "Think it through" and show the mind map.
- Leave questions 2 and 3 for the viewer: "pause the video and try them".

## 8. Close (20 s)
- Rule to remember: must hold every time → code; nice to have → prompt and examples.
- Point to the Trap gym page: the next trap is "The tool picker".
