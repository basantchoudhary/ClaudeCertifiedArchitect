# Beat sheet: "The context meter" (about 8–10 minutes)

Not a script. Glance at the point, then say it in your own words while showing the page.
Record one beat per take, and pause 2 seconds before restarting a sentence; the fillers get cut in editing.

## 1. The hook (30 s)
- Show the title and the lede.
- Say: when a long chat forgets things, two answers always look sensible: a bigger window, a longer summary. Both are the trap.
- Promise: one question that tells you which of four real fixes the exam wants.

## 2. The hospital handover (1 min)
- Scroll to the two boxes. Retelling: "40 mg at 6 pm" becomes "some pain relief earlier", then nothing.
- The chart at the foot of the bed: nobody retells it, so it stays exact.
- Then the two habits: copy three values, don't staple the printout; allergy box at the top, not page 9.
- Land it: summary = retelling, case-facts block = chart, trimming = copy the values, key facts first = the allergy box.

## 3. Where each fix acts (1 min)
- Point at the diagram left to right: case-facts block (kept word for word), summary (rewritten each pass), latest tool result (trim it).
- Point at the bottom strip: start and end read well, the middle is easy to miss.
- One line to repeat: "A bigger window makes the bar longer. The middle grows with it."

## 4. The one question (1 min)
- "Which facts must come out exact, and where do they live right now?"
- Walk the five bullets, one example each. Show the two word lists: underline these in the stem.

## 5. Simulator (3 min). The heart of the video
- Long refund dispute + bigger context window: no summary pass, but 3 of 5 exact. Two facts overlooked in the middle of a 366k chat, and 22.5M tokens sent versus 11.7M. "Delays it."
- Same situation + case-facts block: 5 of 5 exact, even though there are still 2 summary passes. Read the agent's reply out loud: "$24.00 credit … chicken thighs x2 …".
- Flip back to longer summaries: 2 of 5. "Better retelling is still retelling."
- Chat with many order lookups, no change line: 0 of 5 exact. Read the bad reply: "I've added a peanut satay kit". Then trim tool results: meter stays at 84k, 0 passes, 5 of 5, 2.3M tokens.
- One long input that fits + bigger window: identical to no change, 3 of 5. Then key facts first + headers: 5 of 5.
- Long Claude Code session + /compact: 5 of 5. Then point out /compact is "Not available here" in the support chat.
- Say once: token sizes and blur rates are illustrative; the shape is what the exam guide says.

## 6. The three traps table (1 min)
- Read the trap names: more room, better retelling, right fix for the wrong problem. One example each from the runs you just did.

## 7. Practice question 1, live (2 min)
- Read the stem. Point at "about forty fields" and "uses only the items, the delivery date, the status and the price".
- Rule out by naming traps: bigger window delays it, longer summary delays it, case-facts block is a different problem.
- Click the answer, open "Think it through", show the mind map.
- Leave questions 2 and 3 for the viewer: "pause the video and try them".

## 8. Close (20 s)
- Rule: facts that must stay exact go where no summary touches them; cut what nobody reads; key points first. More room is never the fix.
- Next trap: "Extraction honesty".
