Plain questions, no clean winner

This is Mock #7 rewritten after sitting the real exam. Two things about the real exam shaped it:

  • The questions are not cryptic. Each one tells the whole situation in plain words: what happens today, what goes wrong or what is wanted, then the question. No riddle-like clue sentences.
  • There is no clean winner. All four options are real practices that would work to some degree. The answer is the one that fits this situation best, as the exam guide recommends.

When an answer is revealed, the key says “Best.” and every other option says “Works, but …” with the reason it loses: overbuilt, only usually works, wrong home, too late, and so on. Those reasons are the exam's traps; the Trap gym teaches them one at a time.

Every question also has Explain simply and Think it through: the assumption to fix, a mind map, the question's key sentences quoted, and the rule to remember with its exam-guide line.
